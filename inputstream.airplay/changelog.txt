v0.1.6
- Mirroring runs at the screen's frame rate rather than the library's default
  of 30, which is what a sender was being told to encode for.
- A sender that goes away without saying so -- out of range, out of battery,
  locked -- is noticed in fifteen seconds rather than two minutes or never.
- A password or pairing requirement is advertised, so a sender knows about it
  before it is challenged.
- Accepts H.265 mirroring, which is what a phone sends to a 4K display. Without
  it mirroring failed outright on those.
- The receiver writes to the add-on from a thread of its own, so a slow
  consumer no longer stalls volume, progress and format negotiation.
- Video handed over from a browser starts where the phone was, plays rather
  than sitting paused, and is reported honestly while it is still opening.
- Stopping playback locally is noticed, so the phone stops showing a video
  Kodi is not playing.
- Remote-control commands no longer block the service loop.

v0.1.5
- Fixes a framing error that could take Kodi down with it.
- The socket moves to Kodi's temp directory, which fits inside the length
  limit macOS puts on one; cover art and paired devices stay in the profile.
- UxPlay is declared as an add-on dependency rather than downloaded during
  configure.
- Says so in the log if Kodi's own AirPlay receiver is enabled too.

v0.1.4
- Fixes the settings screen's "forget paired devices" button, and the
  transport commands a keymap can raise, which 0.1.3 stopped delivering.

v0.1.3
- Works when installed from a distribution package, not just a zip.
- The receiver socket is owner-only, and cover art no longer needs /run.
- Verbose logging and forgetting paired devices are settings.
- Remote control still works with Kodi's remote control service turned off.
- Fixes a race on the session state between the receiver's threads.

v0.1.2
- macOS support.
- The receiver socket moved from /run into the add-on's profile directory.

v0.1.1
- Build against a Kodi without the live edge patches, and against a linker
  that discards libm before playfair needs it.

v0.1.0
- Initial release: screen mirroring, video streamed from apps, and audio.
- Sender volume control, PIN pairing, remote control over DACP, and elapsed
  time and duration for streamed audio.
